# What should selection reward in the delayed food-cue study?

The original wiring study ranked genomes by the correctness and margin of a turn after a cue disappeared. Speed and feeding remained native actions, but neither contributed to that score. In the reproductive assay a founder has to retain the cue, turn toward the right patch, travel far enough, activate eating on the next tick and gain enough energy to reproduce. The transferred controllers receive the early cue as their sole external neural input. They cannot sense that algae appeared under them or use a hunger signal to trigger eating. This is a useful control for recall and a limitation on interpreting feeding behavior. ## Findings that change the experiment

- In replicate fruit-fly populations, Dunlap and Stephens manipulated both the reliability of experience and how often the best egg-laying choice changed. The evolutionary response depended on the statistical relationship between experience and its consequences. For Terrarium this motivates linking selection to realized food and offspring and comparing reliable cues with a matched shuffle. Fly associative learning and a digital controller retaining a cue over a few ticks are different processes. [Dunlap and Stephens, Proceedings of the Royal Society B (2009)](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2009.0602)
- Jennings and colleagues measured food seeking and consumption separately in mice and found individual recorded neurons that preferentially responded during one or the other. For Terrarium the actionable implication is to score arrival and actual intake separately. The mouse study supplies no reason to install a mammalian hunger circuit or interpret the sign of an output as subjective hunger. [Jennings et al., Cell (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2014.12.026)
- Robira and colleagues simulated foragers with different ways of using past information about food availability. Recent sampling could approach the performance of more elaborate temporal memory under some resource schedules. A simple strategy deserves a fair baseline here too. Always choosing one patch and eating there already captures half the meals on the balanced schedule. It should be evaluated with the same energetic accounting as a memory-using founder. [Robira et al., Royal Society Open Science (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsos.210809)

## Direct feeding check

We replayed the full donor controller through the feeding tick for all eight earlier five-blank passers in the close, predictive, intact condition. At the trained delay they turned correctly on all 32 opportunities and landed on food 26 times. For the eight consumed meals the eating output was positive. For the 18 other correct landings it was strictly negative. All 18 had a full algae pulse and room to absorb the energy. The same 26/8/18 pattern recurred at 11 blanks. At 2 and 8 blanks there were no correct landings to rescue. These are repeated observations from the same eight controllers. [Reproducible gate audit](../../analysis/food_cue_feeding_gate.py)

## Would guaranteed feeding solve it?

We first reconstructed the actual reserves and births at all 1,984 close-gap events for the intact and reset founders in both cue conditions. We then credited a full meal at every correct landing, holding the native trajectories and energy costs fixed and recomputing births. With predictive cues the number of founders gaining births from memory rose from 1/31 to a projected 4/31. Under shuffled cues it rose from 0/31 to 2/31. Among the eight earlier recall passers, one had a projected advantage with predictive cues, but none had a greater advantage under predictive cues than under shuffled cues. This is an accounting projection on fixed behavior, not a newly evolved policy. Feeding failures explain only part of the reproductive shortfall. Run `python3 analysis/food_cue_meal_counterfactual.py` to reproduce the individual contrasts. [Counterfactual implementation](../../analysis/food_cue_meal_counterfactual.py)

## Next local experiment

Start fresh matched founders and vary two things independently. Train at a fixed delay or at variable delays with the same mean and equal evaluation budgets. Within each delay condition, rank one arm by turn correctness as before and the other by direct offspring from native movement and feeding. Keep the cue interface, genomes, patch geometry and mutation rules common. Freeze the delay schedules, endpoints and treatment of ties before training. Check that the reproductive score distinguishes candidates at the start.

At evaluation, compare each locked controller with its own state cleared and with cues shuffled under identical food deliveries. Use fresh schedules and both cue signs, retain failures and report turns, landings and meals alongside births. Include the always-choose-one-side baseline. A convincing gain would be more offspring with memory intact on reliable cues, replicated across independent founders, with a smaller or absent gain after shuffling. If reward alignment improves feeding but turns still reverse at unseen delays, the next target is timing generalization. If simple fixed behavior does just as well, the environment has supplied little selective advantage for recall.
