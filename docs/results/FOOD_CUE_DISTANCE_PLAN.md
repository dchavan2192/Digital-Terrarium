# Can the remembered turn lead to more births when both patches are reachable?

Use the same three saved controllers and the complete seed-6400 food schedule from the transfer study. Repeat the original 0.25-unit starting distance, halve it once and twice (0.125 and 0.0625), and double it (0.5). These distances were chosen after seeing the previous study's movement ranges. Only the founder's starting y-coordinate changes. Keep the left/right boundary, cue, delay, movement budget, body size, feeding and reproduction unchanged.

For each controller and distance report direct births with intact memory versus a pre-choice reset in the reliable environment. Also report shuffled cues and visible rescue. Retain all 16 opportunities per lifetime including failures. Count food-cell landings, actual meals and births separately. Check that turn, horizontal position and forward displacement match across distances, and that the original distance reproduces the previously archived event records. These are repeated interventions on three controllers from two founding populations. They can identify an effect of reachability within these cases. They cannot establish prevalence across evolved populations.
