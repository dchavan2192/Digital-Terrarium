# Local transfer of three archived memory controllers

Replay the three controllers previously identified as sustaining recall at every delay from 0 through 32. They are the selected seed-6226 champion (ID 295) from the wiring study and the fixed- and variable-delay seed-6320 champions (IDs 297 and 302) from the delay-training study. The latter two share a founding population. This case series is a transfer check for known recall competence.

Use schedule 6400 and the calibrated two-patch world. Each controller supplies all seven native actions, including its own speed and eating intent. Standardize body size to one. Map the single original input to the native heard-signal channel and preserve recurrent connections, biases, weights and activation order. Verify all seven actions before and after sensor remapping for both cue signs, all four delays and all three interventions. Compare recorded turns in the environment to the archived stationary evaluator.

For each donor run six lifetimes crossing predictive or shuffled cues with intact memory, a pre-choice memory reset, or the reset plus a visible cue at choice. Use every scheduled event. Report landings on the food cell, meals consumed and unique direct newborns. A choice reaching neither cell and a correct landing without consumption both remain in the denominator. Check the native energy ledger and the parent and tick of each birth. Abort and report a failed assay if a founder dies, a birth is blocked, or a control or ledger check fails. With this bounded pilot, an incomplete lifetime cannot support the paired endpoint.

The primary descriptive contrast is the predictive-minus-shuffled birth count with memory intact, compared to that difference after reset. Report each donor individually. A positive difference can show that a previously evolved circuit transfers to this controlled environment.
