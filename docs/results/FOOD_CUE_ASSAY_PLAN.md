# Local validation of a delayed food cue and direct offspring endpoint

This engineering study asks whether the native engine can turn retained information into a feeding opportunity and a counted birth. The unit is a founder's lifetime on a specified 16-event schedule. Repeated meals and its children are nested observations.

Begin one founder at 45 energy. Reset pose and neural activations for each event, preserving age and energy. Hold movement and turning for one cue tick and 2, 5, 8 or 11 blank ticks. During the choice tick the animal takes one native step north from the boundary between two adjacent columns. Positive turn points left and negative turn right. Both patches are empty until that step commits. At the next tick the scheduled patch receives one unit of algae worth 15 energy. Lock movement for this meal and remove unconsumed algae afterward. Native metabolism, feeding and asexual reproduction operate during all phases. The threshold is 60, parent investment 30 and newborn endowment 30. Register direct newborn IDs and remove them before the next tick. This measures direct births of a solitary founder in a controlled foraging arena.

For each delay include all four ordered combinations of food sign and shuffled cue sign once. Randomize event order with a seed separate from the world's RNG. This gives the predictive arm 16/16 reliable cues and the shuffled arm 8/16, with identical food location, amount and tick in each pair. Use the same founder seed and weights across paired arms. The only sensory connections in the validation controllers are the cue, bias and optional neural feedback. Verify the held pose, unavailable food at choice, reward timing, energy accounting and direct parent IDs. Use intact recurrent, single pre-choice reset, matched visible-cue reset and feed-forward manufactured controls. Require perfect predictive choices from intact and visible rescue and chance-level food hits from hidden reset and feed-forward controls. Compare lifetime births under predictive and shuffled cues in every policy. This is instrument calibration with fixed policies and chosen energetic coefficients.

Implement a bounded single-schedule CLI with immutable session outputs. Local development fixtures may exercise additional schedule seeds. Do not infer discovery by natural selection or competitive fitness from validation. A replicated Pi experiment requires a later prospective design, suitable measured compute budget and separate authorization.
