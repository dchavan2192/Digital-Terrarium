# Local factorial pilot of delay training and reproductive selection

Use eight fresh independent founder-population seeds 6700–6707. Within each seed use the same 16 initially feed-forward genomes for all four arms. Cross fixed versus varied training delays with selection for native turn or actual direct offspring. Fix the starting gap at 0.0625. Every candidate receives one predictive-cue lifetime on balanced schedule seed 6800. Four blocks have delays [5,5,5,5] or [3,4,6,7], with equal mean delay and identical ordered cue signs across timing arms. Each block includes both food signs crossed with both shuffled signs. All movement, feeding and reproductive costs use the established native arena.

Before evolution, require at least two distinct founder birth scores within at least four of eight populations for each timing regime. Failure stops the pilot before training. Over 12 generations retain the best four and generate 12 children per population per generation with the previous wiring study's mutation settings (recurrent addition 0.3). Rank turn-selected candidates by strictly correct signed turns, then minimum signed margin. Rank birth-selected candidates by direct births only. Break remaining ties by ascending candidate ID. This procedure is artificial selection on a measured endpoint.

Lock each final top-ranked genome before testing. Evaluate at unseen delays 2, 5, 8 and 11 using two fresh balanced orders (6801 and 6802). Preserve all six combinations of cue reliability and state intervention. Include the constant-side feed-forward reference and the engineered recurrent reference at the same gap. For each donor, average its paired direct-birth differences across the two orders. Primary benefit is intact minus reset with reliable cues. Report the shuffled difference and their difference to distinguish cue-specific benefit from a generic state effect. Compare the four treatments within founding population, with turn, landing, consumption and birth outcomes retained. Report all eight founders and retain all ties and losses. This pilot estimates feasibility and gives descriptive paired effects. A replicated Pi campaign is a later decision informed by measured runtime, an informative signal and device readiness.
