# Paired selection and delay transfer

Use 32 fresh founder seeds 6100–6131. Each pair shares all 16 founders. Use the stationary pilot's supplied three weights, training delay five, two signed cues, weight bounds and mutation law. In each of 16 generations keep four adults and produce 12 children. In the selected arm retain the four highest-ranked adults (correct count, worst signed margin, then lowest ID). In the neutral arm retain four uniformly drawn without replacement. Sample parents uniformly among the retained four in both arms. Pair the parent-slot draws and mutation increments using identical RNG streams after founder generation. Use a separate seeded stream for neutral survivor draws.

Primary endpoint is the paired difference in generation-16 fraction scoring 2/2 on hidden cues, averaged equally across all 32 pairs. Record every generation, first success including generation zero, discovery among founder-negative populations, and the number of newly produced children scoring 2/2 among children of successful parents. Lock the best final training-ranked controller per arm even for neutral runs. Assess both champions at delays 1, 10 and 20 plus training delay 5 with hidden/visible cues and intact/boundary/every-tick resets. Define transfer success at each delay as 2/2 intact hidden, 1/2 boundary-reset hidden, and 2/2 boundary-reset visible. Report paired directions, all denominators and failures. This descriptive comparison estimates the effect of the specified survivor policy in the supplied circuit. The deliberate best-final readout measures best available capability, while population prevalence measures spread.
